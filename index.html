About Us

A Boutique Legal Practice Built Around Strategy

Sanन्याय is a boutique legal practice founded by Advocate Sanyam Rastogi, providing legal counsel, litigation representation, dispute resolution, advisory and legal documentation to individuals, businesses and institutions. The practice combines the focused attention of a boutique practice with the capabilities of an extensive professional team.

At Sanन्याय, every matter begins with understanding. We examine the facts, documents, legal position, practical considerations and objectives involved before developing a course of action. Our approach is centred around clear advice, careful preparation and purposeful representation.

Advocate Sanyam Rastogi, Founder
